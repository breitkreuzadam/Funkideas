▲ Everest Scale

Snap any mountain. We'll stand Mt. Everest behind it, at the same scale, so you can see how they really compare.

  1. Take a picture of a mountain.
  2. Drag the summit marker to its peak and the base marker to its foot.
  3. Pick the mountain (or let your GPS find nearby peaks) and its base elevation.
  4. Everest appears behind it, to scale. Save or share the result.
Drag ● to the summit and ● to the base. Drag anywhere else to slide Everest.
Look & feel

Assumes Everest stands the same distance away as your mountain, measured from the same base elevation. Everest: 8,849 m (29,032 ft).
